## Long description (paste into itch)

```
Fit the pieces. Burst the lines. Keep the board breathing.

Lineburst is an endless 8×8 grid puzzle. Each turn you get three shapes. Drag them on. Fill a full row or column and it bursts. Combos square. A boxed-in clear is a tight fit. Chain hard clears for back-to-back pay.

**Play**
- Drag a piece onto the board, or tap a piece then tap a cell (places from the top-left).
- Pieces do not rotate. The shape you see is the shape you place.
- After all three are down, a new set arrives.

**Skill**
- **Hold** — tap a piece, then Hold (or press C). One swap until you place. A parked piece can still save a dead tray.
- **Tight fit** — a clear that boxes the piece in pays extra.
- **Back-to-back** — arm B2B with a tight fit, 2+ lines, or a perfect clear. The next hard clear pays ×1.5. Easy singles break it.

**Survive**
Three undos per run. Watch a short break to keep the board, shuffle a dead tray, or refill an undo. High score is stored on this device.

No account. No tutorial wall. One more drop.
```
